# Car-Market-Analysis-Using-Web-Scraping-and-Data-Analytics
Used Car Market Analysis in Hyderabad
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the used car market in Hyderabad by collecting real-world data from CarDekho through web scraping. The dataset was cleaned, processed, and analyzed to uncover trends related to car prices, vehicle age, mileage, transmission type, and other important factors affecting the resale value of cars.

The project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow, including data collection, cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical testing, and visualization.

🎯 Objectives
Collect used car data from CarDekho Hyderabad listings.
Clean and preprocess the scraped dataset.
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
Analyze factors affecting used car prices.
Compare Automatic and Manual transmission vehicle prices.
Identify relationships between price, vehicle age, and mileage.
Generate meaningful business insights through visualizations.

📈 Key Findings
Newer vehicles generally have higher resale prices.
Vehicle Year shows a positive correlation with Price.
KM Driven has a weak relationship with Price.
Automatic cars tend to have higher average prices than Manual cars.
Vehicle age is one of the strongest factors influencing used car prices.
💡 Business Insights
Buyers can identify better-value vehicles based on age and mileage.
Dealers can use pricing trends to optimize resale strategies.
Vehicle age significantly impacts market value.
Market demand influences the pricing of automatic transmission vehicles.
